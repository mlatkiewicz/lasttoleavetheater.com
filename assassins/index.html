ASSASSINS — Last to Leave Theater Company

November 6 – 29, 2026

Fri & Sat 8pm • Sun 2pm

The Other Space

West Hollywood

A COMMUNITY ASSEMBLY ON GRIEVANCE IN AMERICA.

Attendance is mandatory.

In observance of the nation's semiquincentennial, we present Stephen Sondheim and John Weidman's Assassins as a school assembly. Nine members of this community have been selected to research and personify the nine Americans who shot, or tried to shoot, a president. The school offers the assembly as an exercise in national rehabilitation.

You will sit inside the classroom, under the presidential portraits, around the desks and chalkboard. Fifty seats. No stage.

The lesson does not go as intended.
